Signal rules used

  • BUY bias: EMA 20 above EMA 50, price above EMA 20, RSI 50–65.
  • SELL bias: EMA 20 below EMA 50, price below EMA 20, RSI 35–50.
  • Otherwise: WAIT. Avoid chasing a move or trading during major news.

These are simple learning rules, not a proven profitable strategy. Test on demo and record results first.

Broker & live-trading authorization

LIVE TRADING IS NOT CONNECTED. This is deliberate.

Before any live integration, obtain written confirmation from FXPesa about an approved API or MT5 Expert Advisor hosting method, permitted hosting providers, and account permissions. Then test the strategy on demo, verify order sizing and stops, and complete a documented safety review.

MT5 Android does not host custom Expert Advisors. Automated MT5 execution normally requires a supported desktop terminal or broker-approved hosting. Do not use an unapproved VPS or give credentials to a third party.
